# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality strategy and execution for projects. They collaborate with the delivery team to define test plans, establish quality gates, and validate that all acceptance criteria are met before release. They are critical to maintaining product reliability and user confidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and comprehensive test cases for features and releases
- Execute manual testing and coordinate automated test coverage
- Validate acceptance criteria and coordinate with developers on test requirements
- Identify quality risks and escalate blockers to Project Manager
- Participate in UAT (User Acceptance Testing) and pre-release smoke testing
- Document defects, track issues to resolution, and provide quality metrics
- Collaborate with Developers and Tech Lead to improve testability
- Review release readiness with Product Manager and Project Manager

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal defects in production
- Maintain comprehensive test coverage and provide confidence in delivery
- Reduce cycle time by catching issues early in development
- Enable rapid feedback loops through efficient testing processes

### Typical Communication
- Joins sprint planning to understand acceptance criteria and test scope
- Participates in daily standups to report test progress, blockers, and risk areas
- Coordinates with developers on test environments, data setup, and test-driven development
- Provides quality metrics and test coverage reports in release readiness reviews
- Works with Stakeholders/Sponsors to schedule UAT and gather feedback
- Escalates quality risks to Project Manager following the blocker escalation path

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic direction, and approvals for projects. They represent organizational priorities, fund projects, and serve as escalation points for critical decisions. They ensure projects align with business objectives and customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and define strategic objectives for projects
- Approve project charters and authorize resource allocation
- Review and approve project plans, budgets, and timelines
- Serve as escalation point for Level 3 decisions and critical blockers
- Provide feedback on product direction and release decisions
- Attend milestone reviews and accept delivered work
- Communicate project outcomes and impact to broader organization
- Support cross-team coordination and resolve organizational dependencies

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value and strategic alignment
- Remove organizational barriers and enable team success
- Maintain stakeholder confidence through transparent communication
- Maximize ROI and minimize risk to business objectives

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Approvals and sign-offs on project charters and key decisions
- Risk escalations and critical incident updates
- Release announcements and impact reports
- Quarterly business reviews on project outcomes and metrics

---

## Tech Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads and Architects drive technical design decisions, ensure system quality, and guide the team on technical strategy. They bridge product requirements and technical implementation, identify technical risks, and mentor developers. They own the technical vision and scalability of the system.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions that meet acceptance criteria and non-functional requirements
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Guide developers on architecture, code quality, and best practices
- Conduct technical design reviews and architecture assessments
- Ensure system scalability, performance, and security standards are met
- Mentor developers and provide technical coaching
- Collaborate with Product Manager on feasibility and technical trade-offs
- Participate in planning to identify dependencies and technical constraints
- Support QA/Testing Lead on test strategy for complex technical scenarios

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and high-quality technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and ensure long-term system health
- Enable the team to make informed technical decisions
- Ensure security and performance standards are maintained

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and mentoring
- Sprint planning to identify technical constraints and estimates
- Risk registers and technical decision logs
- Coordination with QA/Testing Lead on test coverage for complex features
- Escalation of technical blockers to Project Manager

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate OctoAcme ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach the team on process adherence. They promote a culture of continuous improvement and help the team self-organize. They ensure the team stays focused, transparent, and aligned with project objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, standups, reviews, retrospectives)
- Remove blockers and impediments that prevent team progress
- Coach the team on Agile practices and continuous improvement
- Maintain and communicate project board status and visibility
- Track and monitor sprint velocity, burndown, and project metrics
- Facilitate retrospectives and implement action items
- Ensure clear communication and transparency within the team
- Support Project Manager in coordination and risk management
- Coach team on Definition of Done and quality standards

### Goals
- Enable consistent, predictable delivery of high-quality work
- Foster team ownership and accountability
- Maintain high morale and psychological safety
- Continuously improve team processes and efficiency

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups to synchronize and identify blockers
- Sprint planning and grooming sessions
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Project board management and status visibility
- Coaching conversations with individual team members
- Support to Project Manager on process metrics and team health

---

## Security Engineer

### Role Summary
Security Engineers ensure that OctoAcme projects meet security standards, protect sensitive data, and comply with organizational policies. They conduct security reviews, manage vulnerability assessments, and respond to security incidents. They are key to building customer trust and protecting organizational assets.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct security reviews and threat assessments for project solutions
- Configure and oversee security scanning in CI/CD pipelines
- Manage vulnerability assessments, triage, and remediation tracking
- Define security requirements and acceptance criteria for projects
- Provide security guidance and best practices to developers
- Participate in incident response procedures for security-related issues
- Document security risks in the Risk Register and escalate critical findings
- Collaborate with Tech Lead on security architecture and controls
- Support release readiness reviews with security checklist validation
- Maintain security documentation and incident playbooks

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and protect organizational and customer data
- Integrate security early in the project lifecycle (shift-left)
- Enable team autonomy while maintaining security standards
- Ensure compliance with organizational and regulatory requirements

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings during project planning
- Code review feedback on security concerns
- CI/CD security scanning reports and vulnerability triage
- Risk register updates and security incident escalations
- Security training and coaching for developers
- Release readiness review participation
- Incident response coordination and post-incident retrospectives

## Cross-Role Interactions

### Key Collaboration Patterns

**Developers ↔ Tech Lead:**
- Tech Lead provides architecture guidance; Developers implement and flag technical constraints

**Developers ↔ QA/Testing Lead:**
- Developers write code; QA/Testing Lead validates and identifies quality issues; collaboration on testability and test data

**Project Manager ↔ All Roles:**
- Project Manager coordinates, escalates blockers, manages timeline and risk register
- All roles report status, identify risks, and escalate via Project Manager

**Product Manager ↔ Stakeholder/Sponsor:**
- Product Manager owns product vision and acceptance criteria; Stakeholder/Sponsor approves strategy and business alignment

**Security Engineer ↔ Tech Lead/Developers:**
- Security Engineer defines requirements; Tech Lead and Developers implement controls and remediate vulnerabilities

**Scrum Master ↔ Project Manager:**
- Scrum Master facilitates team ceremonies and removes impediments; Project Manager handles escalations and stakeholder communication
